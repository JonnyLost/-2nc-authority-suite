# 2NC Authority Suite v4.26.0 — Rap, R&B & Electronic Expansion

This release completes a deep retail-coverage audit of Rap / Hip-Hop, R&B / Soul, and electronic music while preserving the established Authority taxonomy and browse structure.

## v4.26.0 changes

- Added 401 previously missing artists: 127 Rap / Hip-Hop, 123 R&B / Soul, and 151 electronic artists filed under Alternative.
- Assigned 148 Essential and 253 Recommended authority levels after a final retail-usefulness review.
- Expanded EDM, Dubstep, House, Techno, Trance, Drum & Bass, IDM, Electronica, and related electronic styles without creating new top-level genres.
- Added a primary and secondary subgenre to every new artist so vinyl and CD labels retain both requested descriptor lines.
- Preserved all 6,296 existing music records and IDs, locally edited Authority Manager records, retired/deleted records, comics, queues, labels, printing, synchronization, and offline behavior.
